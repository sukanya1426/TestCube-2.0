## What the app does

Fossify Voice Recorder exposes 23 documented user-facing features, including record audio, pause and resume a recording, watch the live audio level while recording, continue recording in the background, browse saved recordings, play a recording.

- Record audio
- Pause and resume a recording
- Watch the live audio level while recording
- Continue recording in the background
- Browse saved recordings
- Play a recording
- Rename a recording
- Delete a recording
- Restore or empty the recycle bin
- Share a recording
- View recording properties
- Search recordings
- Sort the recordings list
- Choose the recording audio format
- Set the recording bitrate and quality

- Hide the recording notification
